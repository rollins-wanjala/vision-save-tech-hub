MEET THE FOUNDER

Orupia Rollins Wanjala

Founder & CEO | Vision Save Hub Tech

Vision Save Hub Tech was born from a simple belief that technology should create opportunities. Growing up, I experienced many challenges and I was blessed by people who believed in me and supported my education. Their kindness inspired me to dream bigger.

Today, my vision is to build an ICT hub where young people can learn digital skills, enjoy technology, access quality ICT services and gain confidence to start businesses of their own.

Every customer, every student and every sponsor becomes part of this journey of transforming lives through technology.

Technology That Creates Opportunities
