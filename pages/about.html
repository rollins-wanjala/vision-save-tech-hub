OUR STORY

A Dream Built Through Hope

Vision Save Hub Tech started from a dream of using technology to create opportunities for young people. Growing up, I experienced many challenges but I was blessed by people who believed in me and supported my education.

That support changed my life and inspired me to build a technology hub that trains others in computer skills, provides quality ICT services and helps people discover their potential.

Today, our vision is to become one of the most trusted ICT hubs in Nairobi while giving back to the community through technology education, mentorship and digital empowerment.

Our Mission

To provide quality ICT services, practical computer training and affordable technology solutions while creating opportunities that empower learners, businesses and the community through innovation and digital skills.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted ICT hub in Nairobi known for excellence in technology training, customer service and community empowerment.
